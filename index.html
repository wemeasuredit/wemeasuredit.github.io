Claims about data tools, tested on examples small enough to follow.

Each guide takes a claim somebody is making, runs it on a problem you can hold in your head, and says plainly how the numbers came out — including the times they came out badly. Everything here was measured rather than repeated.

Getting answers from a spreadsheet, without an expert and without much data

Google released TabFM, a model that claims to make predictions from a table it has never seen, using as few as ten example rows. We invented a dataset where the right answer is known exactly, so there is a ceiling to measure against, and ran TabFM against the two methods it would have to beat.
